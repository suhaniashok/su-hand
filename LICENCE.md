## Free for small indie apps

If you are an individual or a very small independent team, you can use Su Hand
for your app for free until it passes **10,000 total downloads**, counted across
every platform and store together, free and paid.

Free means everything except a logo:

- **The app itself.** Setting text in the interface and bundling the font file
  inside the app so it renders.
- **Marketing the app.** Screenshots, store listings, your app's website, promo
  videos, captions, annotations, title cards, social posts, even paid ads.
- **On the web**, load it from the CDN link in the README rather than hosting
  your own copy of the file.

In return:

- **Credit me** in the app (an about or credits screen is fine) and on the app's
  website: "Su Hand font by Suhani Ashok".
- **Tell me when you pass 10,000 downloads.** Open an issue on this repository.
  I can't see your numbers, so I am trusting you.
- **Share your count if I ask.** I may ask for your total downloads now and
  then, and you agree to share them from App Store Connect, Google Play Console,
  or wherever your app is distributed.

## App or marketing?

This only matters once you are paying. One question decides it: **who put the
text there?**

- **The app did.** That is the App licence. The font is showing because your
  app renders it, including in plain screenshots and screen recordings of the
  app.
- **You did.** That is the Marketing licence. Anything you set in Su Hand
  yourself, in a video editor, Canva, Figma or anywhere else: captions,
  annotations, title cards, headlines, ads, social posts, merchandise, print.
  It does not matter whether the app is on screen.

| Example | Licence |
|---|---|
| Your app's interface set in Su Hand | App |
| A plain screen recording or screenshot of the app | App |
| Su Hand captions or arrows added over that recording | Marketing |
| A Su Hand headline on your App Store screenshots | Marketing |
| A title card, an ad, a social post, a t-shirt | Marketing |

- **Logos.** Su Hand cannot be used in a logo, wordmark, or any brand mark, at

- Selling the font, or passing the letters off as yours.
- Redistributing the font files on their own, hosting your own copy for others

- Bundling it into a template, theme, or font pack sold to other people.

If you are not sure which of these you fall under, open an issue and ask. I will
almost certainly say yes to something.
